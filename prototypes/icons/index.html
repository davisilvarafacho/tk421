CURRENT

Two families in one font: square Font Awesome (power, bell, mic, keyboard, Bluetooth) beside round Material Design (volume, network, media). Weights and optical sizes do not match.

MATERIAL SHARP

Square terminals and right angles, thin at weight 300. Filled variants mark the active state without a second shape. Official Arch package ttf-material-symbols-variable; no dedicated CPU glyph (chip used).

PHOSPHOR LIGHT

Even 1.5 px line, rounded ends, softer and more friendly. Has CPU and GPU glyphs. No Arch package: the font would be copied into the repo.
